Monday, November 9, 2026
November 2 to November 6, 2026

Ethanol at Argo, on a slow day, traded higher on Monday of last week closing at $1.89 per gallon, up 1 and ½ cents per gallon from the previous Friday’s close. D6 and D4 RINs traded
higher on Monday of last week in a slower session. D6 RINS closed at $1.79 per RIN, up 2 cents per RIN from the previous Friday’s close. Meanwhile, D4 RINs closed at $1.90 per RIN, up 3
cents per RIN from the previous Friday’s close. LCFS credits in California closed at $91 per MT, up $1 per MT from the previous Friday’s close.
In a very active day in renewables on Tuesday of last week, LCFS credits in California closed at $91 per MT, no change day-over-day. Ethanol at Argo closed out the day on Tuesday of last
week at $1.88 per gallon, down1 cent per gallon day-over-day. RINs traded higher. D6 RINs closed at $1.81 per RIN, up 2 cents per RIN day-over-day. Meanwhile, D4 RINs closed at $1.92
per RIN, also up 2 cents per RIN, day-over-day.
In another busy session in RINs on Wednesday of last week, RINs continued to trade higher. D4 RINs closed at $1.96 per RIN, up 4cents per RIN day-over-day. Meanwhile, D6 RINs closed at
$1.84 per RIN, up 3 cents per RIN day-over-day. LCFS credits in California closed at $90 per MT, down $1 per MT day-over-day. Ethanol at Argo closed at $1.86 per gallon, down 2 cents per
gallon day-over-day.
Despite falling corn prices on Thursday of last week, Ethanol at Argo closed higher, up two cents per gallon day over day closing at $1.88 per gallon. RINs in a slower session closed
mixed. D6 RINS closed at $1.84 per RIN, no change day-over-day. Meanwhile, D4 RINs closed at $1.94 per RIN down 2 cents per RIN day-over-day. LCFS credits in California closed at $90
per MT, no change day-over-day.
LCFS credits in California closed out the day (and the week) on Friday of last week, at $90.50 per MT, up 50 cents per MT day-over-day, and up 50 cents per MT week-over-week. Ethanol at
Argo closed out the day (and the week) on Friday of last week at $1.89 per gallon, up 1 cent per gallon day-over-day, and up 1 and ½ cents per gallon week-over-week. RINs, in a semi-active
session to end the week on Friday of last week, traded higher. D4 RINs closed out the day (and the week) at $1.96 per RIN, higher 2 cents per RIN day-over-day, and higher 9 cents per RIN
week-over-week. Meanwhile, D6 RINs closed out the day (and the week) at $1.86 per RIN, also up 2 cents per RIN day-over-day, andup 9 cents per RIN week-over-week.
Have a great week!
